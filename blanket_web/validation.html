What the simulator's numbers rest on

The browser runs a multigroup Monte Carlo on the GPU. Its cross sections come from continuous-energy OpenMC (ENDF/B-VIII.0), and OpenMC is checked against the LIBRA breeding experiments at MIT and against the published ARC design. Each link below is measured separately.

Measured: the LIBRA breeding experiments

About a litre of molten salt beside a 14 MeV D-T generator in MIT's vault, with tritium collected and counted. BABY 1L used a LiF-LiCl surrogate salt (4 runs). FLiBe-1L is FLiBe with natural lithium (2 runs; repository data, no paper yet). The browser runs the crucible stack in free space. OpenMC measures what that drops: the generator tube, lead bricks, supports and table, and the room. That ratio carries the browser result to the full experiment.
