# Repurposed a third time, same investigation, sharper target: rather than
# generic diversity, directly attack the one correlation that's shown up
# on EVERY qi-passing real point found anywhere in the project so far (4
# points, 2 field-period settings, 4 lanes) -- a badly-failing
# edge_magnetic_mirror_ratio (0.48-0.56 vs. the 0.25 limit) despite
# mirror_ratio being easy in general (~35% of the real pool passes it).
# grounded_walk_step_v4.py anchors fibers ONLY at the 3 known qi-passing real
# points (--anchor-x-file, not random dataset draws; --anchor-jitter gives
# each anchor a small cluster of nearby starts) and directly minimizes
# edge_magnetic_mirror_ratio (already a first-class --minimize option this
# script family supports) with ONLY qi enforced as a constraint --
# deliberately not vacuum_well/flux_compression/edge_rotational_transform
# yet, to isolate the two-way question before chasing the full 5/5. A quick
# real-VMEC pilot (15 fibers, 4 rounds) before wiring this into the queue
# already shows the tension directly and cleanly: one ALM step dropped
# mirror_ratio from ~0.53-0.56 to 0.37-0.51 across all 15 fibers, but qi blew
# up ~6-13x past its threshold in every one of them; over the next few
# rounds, as the ALM multiplier pulled qi back toward feasibility, the fibers
# that recovered the most qi margin gave back the most mirror_ratio gain
# (best_real_violation min fell 0.21->0.13). Real, oracle-confirmed evidence
# of a local Pareto trade-off, not a search-coverage artifact -- the open
# question this full run is meant to answer is whether continued ALM
# pressure can find a genuinely better point on that trade-off curve (qi
# feasible AND mirror_ratio materially better than 0.53) or just oscillates
# along the same curve indefinitely.
p2_v4_correlation_gpu1|run_grounded_walk_v4.sh|MAX_ROUNDS=3000 MAX_RETRAINS=40 RETRAIN_EPOCHS=60 BASE_SPLIT=bootstrap_live SCORER_ARCH=half_siren|--scorer-tag reg_half_siren_big_soap_full_s0 --nfp 2 --n-fibers 15 --alm-inner-steps 20 --lr 0.01 --anchor-x-file output/qi_pass_anchors_nfp2.npy --anchor-jitter 0.03 --minimize edge_magnetic_mirror_ratio --obj-ramp-start 0.3 --obj-ramp-end 0.05 --stall-retrain-rounds 15 --seed 7 --constraint qi<=0.0003162
#
# RESULT (nfp=2, above): finished in 12 rounds, driver hit its own success
# condition (qi satisfied, the only enforced constraint) and stopped --
# real, not surrogate. Across the 179 real points explored, qi and
# mirror_ratio correlate at r=-0.95: a clean, near-linear local trade-off
# curve, mr running ~0.37 at the worst-qi end up to ~0.69 at the best-qi
# end. The single point that cleared qi with real margin (0.000274) has
# mirror_ratio 0.689 -- the WORST value recorded anywhere in this project,
# well past even the original 0.53-0.56 anchors. Nothing found beats the
# anchors on both axes at once. Read as a genuine local Pareto wall in this
# specific nfp=2 basin, not a search-coverage gap.
#
