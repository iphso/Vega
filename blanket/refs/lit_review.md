# Blanket simulator — experimental validation data review (2026-10-31)

Scope: measured data to validate (1) multigroup neutron MC, (2) FLiBe thermal-hydraulics, (3) tritium transport/permeation, for an ARC-style W | Inconel 718 VV | FLiBe channel | Be | FLiBe tank blanket.
Excludes items already held (LIBRA BABY 100 mL + 1L runs 1-4, Sorbom 2015, Dark 2026 FESTIM-ARC, FNG-HCPB, Calderoni 2008, arXiv 2603.28991, JUPITER-II Be redox, jlball/arc-nonproliferation) except where new facts were found.

**Verified column:** **V** = I opened the source/data file and checked the stated content. **P** = only the abstract, record page or search snippet was seen; bibliographic details are probably correct but the content was not checked. **U** = unverified (could not open, or the claim comes from a secondary source only).
Local copies are in `refs/lit/` (downloaded as untrusted data; nothing was executed).

## Top-5 to add next

1. **LIBRA FLiBe-1L runs 1-2** (measured FLiBe TBR ±8%, foils, surface vs permeation T split). Rebuild the crucible in our MC with natural Li and match TBR within error; then drive the transport model with the logged temperature history and compare the cumulative release curves. *Effort: 1-2 days.* Flag in the demo: repo data, not yet peer reviewed.
2. **MSRE tritium balance** (CF-71-7-8 Table 1). One-loop system check: production → off-gas / wall permeation / graphite. Show that clean-metal permeability over-predicts wall loss, which is the honest MSRE lesson. *Effort: 1 day.* Caveats: an MSR, not a blanket; oil contamination; redox-dependent T2/TF split.
3. **OKTAVIAN LiF 61 cm leakage spectrum + LLNL Li-6/Li-7/Be pulsed spheres.** Simple spheres, which makes them the cleanest test of our multigroup Li/F/Be cross sections and the Be multiplier. *Effort: 2-3 days* (manual download through the IAEA Cloudflare page, digitise or parse the TOF spectra, collapse to our groups).
4. **HTM FLiBe + Ni-alloy property sets as an uncertainty band**, with HYPERION 2026 as the "why the scatter" reference. Turns a single permeability into a defensible ±range slider. *Effort: 0.5 day* (MIT-licensed tabulated Arrhenius parameters; use Inconel 625/600 as a stated 718 proxy).
5. **Hoffman & Lones ORNL-1777 / Ambrosek 2009** Nu vs Dittus-Boelter (±15%) for the cooling channel, with the Inconel interfacial-film caveat. *Effort: 0.5-1 day* (digitise one table or figure).
