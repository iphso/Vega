How far to trust this map

The model behind the map: the fast model's 1D spray stage (heat and mass transfer, Kelvin–Thomson, Coulomb dispersion, scavenging) ported to a fixed-step GPU march. The corona stage uses onset and current tables from 264 resolved field and corona solves. Charging is calibrated to resolved collection by drop size. Spray is described by drop size and charge as a fraction of the Rayleigh limit, which fits any atomizer. Not modeled: ionic wind, separated flow behind the discs, sparkover from measurement (the sparkover formula is uncalibrated), and serrated or sharpened disc edges (all discs are 0.3 mm thick with a smooth edge).
