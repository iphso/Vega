What this model leaves out

The reduction is measured the way the bench measures it: mist leaving with the corona on over mist leaving with it off. Drops follow the air (or the air plus their own inertia) and charge by field and diffusion charging in the ion field; gravity is off, which is right for a vertical tube. The grid is about 1 mm, so the tip jets are only a few cells wide; drops above 20 µm, the real supply current and the wall film are not in it. On the as-built device it predicts less capture than the bench measured.
